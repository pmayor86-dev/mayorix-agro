OUR PURPOSE. OUR DIRECTION.

Connecting agricultural produce to greater opportunity.

MAYORIX AGRO is being developed as an agricultural produce trading and sourcing business, with a long-term ambition to improve how buyers access produce and how supply reaches markets.

OUR STARTING POINT

Build from real produce trading.

Our immediate business focus is on products such as tomatoes, pepper, okra, garden eggs and seasonal produce. The business model is designed around buying and reselling produce, sourcing items for customer requests, and exploring broader distribution opportunities.

Because produce quality and market conditions vary, our approach is to confirm the product condition, selling unit, availability and price before an order is agreed.

WHAT GUIDES OUR APPROACH

Principles we want customers to experience.

◎

Transparency

Communicate confirmed prices, product condition, applicable fees and quotation validity clearly.

✳

Quality awareness

Recognise that freshness, ripeness, size and visible condition affect the value of agricultural produce.

↗

Customer requirements

Understand the intended use, quantities, delivery needs and specifications behind each request.

▦

Practical trading

Build systems around actual availability, transport costs, perishability and agreed order terms.

⌂

Market connections

Work towards connecting buyers with suitable produce sources as the business develops.

↻

Long-term growth

Develop from direct produce trading towards wider distribution and, potentially, a supplier marketplace.

OUR LONG-TERM VISION

A more connected agricultural marketplace.

The long-term ambition is to expand beyond individual produce transactions towards a wider network that can connect buyers, produce suppliers, distributors and other agricultural businesses.

This is a development vision, not a claim that a nationwide supplier network or marketplace is already operational. We intend to build it step by step, starting with reliable trading processes and a useful digital presence.
