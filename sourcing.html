Our sourcing approach

Every season creates a new opportunity.

Agricultural supply changes with the season. Our approach is to assess available produce and the requirements of each customer before confirming a supply arrangement.

🌱

✓

Quality requirements

Buyers can explain their preferred size, freshness, condition and intended use so requirements can be reviewed before an order is agreed.

📦

Quantity planning

From smaller market quantities to larger business enquiries, the requested volume helps determine what may be feasible.

🚚

Transport planning

The destination, quantity, handling needs and transport arrangements are considered when preparing a quotation.
